## About

I am a PhD candidate at the [School of Computing](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/), [National University of Singapore](https://nus.edu.sg/), advised by [Dr. Jialin Li](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~lijl/).
My research interests are in distributed systems and operating systems, with a focus on datacenter infrastructure.
I also work with [Dr. Antoine Kaufmann](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~antoinek/) on full-system simulation.

Before starting PhD, I received my master's degree from National University of Singapore.
I obtained my bachalor's degree at [Beihang University](https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/), where I worked on operating system projects with [Dr. Lei Wang](https://scse.buaa.edu.cn/info/1078/8384.htm).
