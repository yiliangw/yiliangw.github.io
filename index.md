## About Me

I am a PhD candidate at School of Computing, National University of Singapore, advised by [Jialin Li](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~lijl/).
My research focuses on distributed systems and datacenter networking.
I also work on full-system simulation with [Antoine Kaufmann](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~antoinek/).

Before starting PhD, I received my master's degree from National University of Singapore.
I obtained my bachalor's degree at Beihang University, where I was fortunate to work with [Lei Wang](https://scse.buaa.edu.cn/info/1078/8384.htm) on operating systems research.
